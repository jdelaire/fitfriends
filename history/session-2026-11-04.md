1️⃣ Warm-Up (~10 minutes)

- 30s Cat-cow, slow spinal flexion and extension with hands and knees fixed: https://youtu.be/FLQDiUccsko
- 30s Downward dog, gently raise the hips with soft knees: https://youtu.be/4yeyJATAIns
- 30s Bodyweight lateral lunge, alternate sides equally with the chest facing forward: https://youtu.be/tmhESsZcpDY
- 30s Glute bridge, bilateral lifts with both feet on the floor: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hwrC-ngRsoY
- 30s High knees, easy stationary alternating knee lifts: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cUmfCd-Hznk

Repeat exactly two rounds for five minutes of timed work. Use the remaining five minutes for setup and rehearsal of these same five movements. Effort 3-4/10. Use smaller ranges, shallower lunges or marching high knees as needed. All blocks use bodyweight only, external load 0 kg.

For up to 12 participants, use individual spaces on level, dry, unobstructed ground suitable for hands, knees and lying down. Allow about 2m between centers and increase spacing wherever full arm or leg reach overlaps. Keep movement within each person's space.

Allow five minutes afterward for core demonstrations and setup.

2️⃣ TABATA Core (~12 minutes)

Complete the four movements in order, 20s work then 10s rest after each. Repeat six complete circuits, including the final 10s rest. Four movements x 30s x six circuits = 12 minutes. Counts are pacing targets within each interval; position takes priority.

- Dead bug, about 6 alternating leg extensions total per interval, 3 per side. Arms point upward and the lower back stays supported: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4XLEnwUr1d8
- Reverse Crunch, about 6 controlled pelvic curls per interval. Keep the knees bent and lift the pelvis slightly without swinging overhead: https://youtu.be/i3F0ZQIihf8
- V-ups, about 5 controlled repetitions per interval. Lift and lower together without jerking: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7UVgs18Y1P4
- Superman hold, up to 20s with a small chest and leg lift and neutral neck: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LZoWdePF1NQ

At target pace, six circuits give 36 dead bug extensions, 18 per side, 36 reverse crunches, 30 V-ups and 120s of Superman holds. Reduce counts whenever control fades. Bend the knees or shorten the lever, reduce the pelvic lift, and keep the Superman lift small. Take brief resets within the work interval as needed. Effort 6-7/10 with steady breathing.

Allow four minutes afterward for recovery and strength demonstrations.

3️⃣ EMOM Strength (10 minutes)

Alternate the two movements for five two-minute cycles. Start each prescription on its minute and rest for the remainder. Finish the work by 40s at the latest and stop earlier if position deteriorates.

- Minutes 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9: 6 Air squats. Lower for 3s, pause for 1s and stand in 1s, totaling 30s of work: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rMvwVtlqjTE
- Minutes 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10: Glute bridge, hold the demonstrated top position for 30s with both feet planted, then lower and rest. Keep the ribs down and hips level: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hwrC-ngRsoY

Total 30 squats and 150s of bridge holds. Scale to 4 squats at the same five-second cadence and a 20s bridge hold. Use a shallower squat or lower bridge height as needed. Effort 6-7/10. Keep the trunk steady through the accumulated Superman, bridge and hinge work.

Allow four minutes afterward for recovery, finisher demonstrations and setup.

4️⃣ High-Intensity Finisher (~15 minutes)

AMRAP, 15 minutes on one continuous clock. Repeat the following round in order:

- 6 Push-ups, controlled repetitions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_l3ySVKYVJ8
- 12 Bodyweight good morning repetitions, about 1s hinge and 1s stand with soft knees and hips moving backward: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kdeq15AvUVU
- 40 High knees total, 20 per side, stationary and facing forward: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cUmfCd-Hznk

Each round has 58 repetitions. Score complete rounds plus repetitions in the current round, counted in the listed order. Take short rests as needed within the running clock. Stop at 15:00. Effort 7-8/10 with a sustainable opening pace.

Scale each round to 4 knee push-ups, 8 shorter-range good mornings and 20 marching high knees total, 10 per side, for 32 repetitions. Declare the scale before starting and use it throughout. Keep the hinge controlled and the spine steady.

With the demonstrations and transitions above, the session totals exactly 60 minutes: 10 + 5 + 12 + 4 + 10 + 4 + 15.
