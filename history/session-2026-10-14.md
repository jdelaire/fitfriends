2️⃣ TABATA Core (~12 minutes)

Choose one option. Work 20s, rest 10s, then move to the next exercise. Repeat 6 rounds for 12 minutes.

TABATA Core Option A (12 minutes, 20s work / 10s rest)
- Hollow rock: https://youtu.be/Zsd8qs0Q1Tk
- Tall plank with alternating arm reaches: https://youtu.be/NafeHcgoLbU
- Forearm plank with hip abduction: https://youtu.be/0cP8QcBMGf4
- Crunch: https://youtu.be/wY1zLTSm4VA
Keep the hips square during the plank movements. Shorten the hollow rock by bending the knees if the lower back lifts.

TABATA Core Option B (12 minutes, 20s work / 10s rest)
- Modified sit-up: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_HDZODOx7Zw

Allow 4 minutes afterward for strength demonstrations and recovery.

3️⃣ EMOM Strength (10 minutes)

Choose one option. Minute 1 is movement A; minute 2 is movement B. Repeat for 10 minutes, 5 rounds each. Rest for the remainder of each minute.

EMOM Option A (10-minute EMOM, unilateral step control and trunk isometric)
- Minute 1: 12 Step-ups (6 each leg): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5qjqDHOUh-A
- Minute 2: 30s Forearm plank hold: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4ITM-VMO8eI
Step down under control from a stable fixed step. Keep the plank braced and finish each minute with time to rest.

EMOM Option B (10-minute EMOM, explosive leg effort and upper-body tempo)
- Minute 1: 6 Frog squat jump: https://youtu.be/SPxvbluj6vQ
- Minute 2: 6 Bench Dips with a 3-second lowering: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c3ZGl4pAwZ4
Land softly and reset between jumps. Lower for 3 seconds on each dip; use only stable fixed benches, with two groups of up to 6 starting on opposite minutes when bench space allows.
Allow 4 minutes afterward for recovery and finisher setup.

4️⃣ High-Intensity Finisher (~15 minutes)

Choose one version. With the demonstrations and transitions above, the session totals 60 minutes. Keep each participant in an individual exercise space.

AMRAP (15 minutes, buy-in then rounds)
- Buy-in: 60 Jump rope (single unders): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCuXYrTOMxI
- Then each round: 8 Half burpee: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XzCDSM0cdro
- Then each round: 12 Step-ups (6 each leg): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5qjqDHOUh-A
Complete the buy-in once, then repeat the two round movements until 15 minutes expires. Step down under control from a stable fixed step. Score full rounds and extra repetitions after the buy-in.

For Time (15-minute cap, buy-in and cash-out)
- Buy-in: 80 Lateral jumps: https://youtu.be/wPZP8Bwxplo
- 3 rounds: 12 Bench Dips: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c3ZGl4pAwZ4
- 3 rounds: 16 Reverse lunge to knee drive (8 each leg): https://youtu.be/pa3fq5cLeAA
- Cash-out: 80 Lateral jumps: https://youtu.be/wPZP8Bwxplo
Complete the buy-in, three rounds of dips and lunges, then the cash-out. Count each landing as one jump. Use stable fixed benches and stagger starts if space is limited. Record time or completed repetitions at the cap.

Ladder (15-minute cap, up-down 4-8-12-8-4)
- Frog squat jump: https://youtu.be/SPxvbluj6vQ
- Shoulder taps in plank: https://youtu.be/g3bsSUOn-vo
- Reverse lunges: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ry-wqegeKlE
At each rung perform the listed number of frog squat jumps and twice that number of shoulder taps and reverse lunges. Count taps and lunges as total repetitions, split equally between sides. Land softly, keep the hips square, and record time or completed repetitions at the cap.
