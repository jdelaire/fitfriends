1️⃣ Warm-Up (~10 minutes)
- 30s Bodyweight lateral lunge: https://youtu.be/tmhESsZcpDY
- 30s Reverse lunge to knee drive: https://youtu.be/pa3fq5cLeAA
- 30s Push-up to downward dog: https://youtu.be/hLjeoxjX7uo
- 30s Forward line hops: https://youtu.be/bL8mlpkp9Qo
- 30s Glute bridge: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hwrC-ngRsoY
Repeat 2 rounds. Use the remaining 5 minutes to rehearse these movements with gradually increasing range and pace.
Allow 5 minutes afterward for core demonstrations and setup.

2️⃣ TABATA Core (~12 minutes)

Choose one option. Work 20s, rest 10s, then move to the next exercise. Repeat 6 rounds for 12 minutes.

TABATA Core Option A (12 minutes, 20s work / 10s rest)
- Dead bug: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4XLEnwUr1d8
- Crunch: https://youtu.be/wY1zLTSm4VA
- Leg Raises: https://youtu.be/vMhqt7r3HJ0
- Hollow body hold: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4xRpGgttca8
Keep the lower back supported during the supine movements; shorten the lever by bending the knees when needed.

TABATA Core Option B (12 minutes, 20s work / 10s rest)
- Reverse Crunch: https://youtu.be/i3F0ZQIihf8
- Pike Crunch: https://youtu.be/L9Q8_Zx3Uuc
- V-ups: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7UVgs18Y1P4
- Side plank: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKoN7ZYJhX4
Alternate sides each round for the side plank, giving each side 3 intervals. Use bent knees to shorten the lever when needed.
Allow 4 minutes afterward for strength demonstrations and recovery.

3️⃣ EMOM Strength (10 minutes)

Choose one option. Minute 1 is movement A; minute 2 is movement B. Repeat for 10 minutes, 5 rounds each. Rest for the remainder of each minute.

EMOM Option A (10-minute EMOM, bilateral leg tempo and hip-extension isometric)
- Minute 1: 8 Air squats with a 3-second descent: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rMvwVtlqjTE
Lower for 3s, pause for 1s, and stand in 1s; aim to finish in 40s.
- Minute 2: 30s Glute bridge: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hwrC-ngRsoY
Hold the top position continuously, keeping the ribs down and hips level.

EMOM Option B (10-minute EMOM, horizontal and bench-supported push endurance)
- Minute 1: 8 Push-ups: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_l3ySVKYVJ8
- Minute 2: 8 Bench Dips: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c3ZGl4pAwZ4
Use controlled repetitions and finish within 40s. Scale push-ups to the knees and keep the feet closer for dips. Choose this option only with enough stable fixed-bench space; two groups of up to 6 can start on opposite minutes.
Allow 4 minutes afterward for recovery and finisher setup.

4️⃣ High-Intensity Finisher (~15 minutes)

Choose one version. With the demonstrations and transitions above, the session totals 60 minutes. All versions use bodyweight only and can accommodate up to 12 people in individual exercise spaces.

AMRAP (15 minutes, rep ladder inside AMRAP)
- 2-4-6-8… Reverse lunges: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ry-wqegeKlE
- 2-4-6-8… Push-ups: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_l3ySVKYVJ8
Complete both movements at each rung, adding 2 repetitions to each movement every round until time expires. Lunge repetitions are total, split equally between legs. Break larger sets as needed; score completed rungs plus extra repetitions.

For Time (15-minute cap, 5 fixed rounds)
- 12 Glute bridge: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hwrC-ngRsoY
- 10 Jump squats: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=87ynaOgFplw
- 16 Shoulder taps in plank: https://youtu.be/g3bsSUOn-vo
Complete all three movements in order for 5 rounds. Each shoulder tap counts as 1 repetition, 8 each side. Land softly and keep the hips square during taps. Stop at the cap and record unfinished repetitions if needed.

Ladder (15-minute cap, descending 20-16-12-8-4)
- Forward line hops: https://youtu.be/bL8mlpkp9Qo
- Bodyweight lateral lunge: https://youtu.be/tmhESsZcpDY
- Mountain climbers: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruQ4ZwncXBg
Perform 20 repetitions of each movement, then 16, 12, 8, and 4. Each landing is 1 hop; lunges and knee drives are total repetitions, split equally between sides. Keep the torso facing forward throughout. Finish once through and record completion time, or completed repetitions at the cap.
