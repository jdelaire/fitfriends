3️⃣ EMOM Strength (10 minutes)

Choose one option. Repeat for a total of 10 minutes, 5 rounds each. Rest for the remainder of each minute.

EMOM Option A (10-minute EMOM, upper-body tempo and core endurance)
- Minute 1: 6 Bench Dips with a 3-second lowering: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c3ZGl4pAwZ4
- Minute 2: 35s Forearm plank hold: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4ITM-VMO8eI

EMOM Option B (10-minute EMOM, hip extension and explosive leg power)
- Minute 1: 10 Glute bridge: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hwrC-ngRsoY
Hold the top of each bridge for 2s, then lower for 2s.
- Minute 2: 6 Squat jump: https://youtu.be/YGGq0AE5Uyc
Reset between jumps and land softly. Use a stable fixed bench for Option A.
Allow 4 minutes afterward for recovery and finisher setup.

4️⃣ High-Intensity Finisher (~15 minutes)

Choose one version. With the demonstrations and transitions above, the session totals 60 minutes.

AMRAP (15 minutes, buy-in then rounds)
- Buy-in: 40 alternating Step-ups: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5qjqDHOUh-A
Then repeat the following pair for as many rounds as possible until the 15-minute clock ends:
- 8 Bench Dips: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c3ZGl4pAwZ4
- 20 Lateral jumps: https://youtu.be/wPZP8Bwxplo
Use a stable fixed bench or step. Start participants at different points in the buy-in to share platforms.

For Time (15-minute cap, chipper)
Complete in order. Break large sets as needed.
- 60 Air squats: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rMvwVtlqjTE
- 40 Modified sit-up: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_HDZODOx7Zw
- 20 Box jumps: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=52r_Ul5k03g
Use a stable step around 50 cm. Step down after each jump. Scale box jumps to step-ups if needed.

Ladder (15-minute cap, uneven ascending ladder)
- 2-4-6-8… Frog squat jump: https://youtu.be/SPxvbluj6vQ
- 4-8-12-16… Forward line hops: https://youtu.be/bL8mlpkp9Qo
Complete both movements at each rung, adding 2 frog squat jumps and 4 forward line hops each round. Each landing counts as one hop. Continue until time expires.
