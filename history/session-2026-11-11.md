1️⃣ Warm-Up (5 minutes)

2 rounds. 30s per movement. External load 0 kg.

- Air squats: 10 reps per 30s, 3s per rep: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rMvwVtlqjTE
- Reverse lunges: 10 alternating reps total per 30s, 5 per side, 3s per rep: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ry-wqegeKlE
- Jump rope (single unders): 30 jumps per 30s, one per second: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCuXYrTOMxI
- Glute bridge: 10 reps per 30s, 1s lift / 1s hold / 1s lower: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hwrC-ngRsoY
- Shoulder taps in plank: 10 alternating taps total per 30s, 5 per side, 3s per tap: https://youtu.be/g3bsSUOn-vo

2️⃣ TABATA Core (12 minutes)

6 complete circuits of the four movements in order. 20s work / 10s rest per movement, including the final rest. 24 intervals, 12 minutes. External load 0 kg.

- Crunch: 5 reps per work interval, 2s lift / 2s lower: https://youtu.be/wY1zLTSm4VA
- Forearm plank hold: 20s per work interval: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4ITM-VMO8eI
- Mountain climbers: 20 alternating knee drives total per work interval, 10 per side, 1s per knee drive: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruQ4ZwncXBg
- Reverse plank hold: 20s per work interval: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bnu5b61vqGQ

3️⃣ EMOM Strength (10 minutes)

5 alternating two-minute cycles, 10 minutes total. External load 0 kg.

- Minutes 1, 3, 5, 7, 9: Step-ups, 12 alternating reps total, 6 per lead leg, 3s per complete rep. 36s work / 24s rest: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5qjqDHOUh-A
- Minutes 2, 4, 6, 8, 10: Bench Dips, 8 reps, 3s lowering / 1s press. 32s work / 28s rest: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c3ZGl4pAwZ4

4️⃣ High-Intensity Finisher (15 minutes)

15-minute AMRAP. One buy-in, then repeated rounds within the same continuous clock. External load 0 kg.

Buy-in, once:

- Jump rope (single unders): 120 jumps: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCuXYrTOMxI

AMRAP for the remainder of the 15-minute clock:

- Burpees: 6 reps per round: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TU8QYVW0gDU
- Reverse lunges: 10 alternating reps total per round, 5 per side: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ry-wqegeKlE

Score: completed buy-in jumps, then complete 16-rep rounds plus reps in the current round at 15:00. The 120 buy-in jumps do not count as a round.
