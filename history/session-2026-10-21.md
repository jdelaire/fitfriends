1️⃣ Warm-Up (~10 minutes)
- 30s Bodyweight good morning: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kdeq15AvUVU
- 30s Cat-cow: https://youtu.be/FLQDiUccsko
- 30s Downward dog: https://youtu.be/4yeyJATAIns
- 30s Forward line hops: https://youtu.be/bL8mlpkp9Qo
- 30s Bodyweight squat: https://youtu.be/yzL1543i1-o
Repeat 2 rounds. Use the remaining 5 minutes to rehearse these movements and gradually increase the range and pace. Keep the hips moving backward during good mornings, with soft knees and a controlled torso.
Allow 5 minutes afterward for core demonstrations and setup.

2️⃣ TABATA Core (~12 minutes)

Choose one option. Work 20s, rest 10s, then move to the next exercise. Repeat 6 rounds for 12 minutes.

TABATA Core Option A (12 minutes, 20s work / 10s rest)
- Bird dog: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=40a4wZrnCFs
- Flutter kicks: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8fhsu9-Lu8
- Leg Raises: https://youtu.be/vMhqt7r3HJ0
- Forearm plank with alternating arm reaches: https://youtu.be/EsEryFxDlJg
Keep the support hand and knee planted during each bird dog reach; return to the same position and alternate sides without traveling. Keep the hips square during both reaching movements. Shorten the leg lever by bending the knees when needed to maintain control.

TABATA Core Option B (12 minutes, 20s work / 10s rest)
- Hollow body hold: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4xRpGgttca8
- Flutter kicks: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8fhsu9-Lu8
- Forearm plank with hip abduction: https://youtu.be/0cP8QcBMGf4
- Side plank: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKoN7ZYJhX4
Use a bent-knee hollow position when needed. Alternate the moving leg during hip abduction while keeping the torso facing the ground. Alternate sides each round for the side plank, giving each side 3 intervals.
Allow 4 minutes afterward for strength demonstrations and recovery.

3️⃣ EMOM Strength (10 minutes)

Choose one option. Minute 1 is movement A; minute 2 is movement B. Repeat for 10 minutes, 5 rounds each. Rest for the remainder of each minute.

EMOM Option A (10-minute EMOM, posterior-chain tempo and lateral trunk isometric)
- Minute 1: 8 Bodyweight good morning: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kdeq15AvUVU
- Minute 2: 20s each side Side plank: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKoN7ZYJhX4
Lower for 3s, pause for 1s, and stand in 1s on each good morning, totaling 40s. Keep the same slight knee bend throughout the hinge. Change sides promptly during the plank minute and keep time for rest; use the bent-knee version when needed.

EMOM Option B (10-minute EMOM, upper-body pressing tempo and anterior trunk isometric)
- Minute 1: 8 Push-ups: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_l3ySVKYVJ8
- Minute 2: 30s Hollow body hold: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4xRpGgttca8
Lower each push-up for 3s and press up in 1s, totaling 32s. Use knee push-ups when needed to keep the tempo. Shorten the hollow hold lever with bent knees while keeping the lower back supported.
Allow 4 minutes afterward for recovery and finisher setup.

4️⃣ High-Intensity Finisher (~15 minutes)

Choose one version. With the demonstrations and transitions above, the session totals 60 minutes. Keep each participant in an individual exercise space. The AMRAP and For Time versions need no shared equipment; the Ladder needs a jump rope for each participant.

AMRAP (15 minutes, three 5-minute density blocks)
- Burpees: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TU8QYVW0gDU
- Air squats: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rMvwVtlqjTE
- V-ups: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7UVgs18Y1P4
Minutes 0-5: repeat 6 burpees and 12 air squats for as many rounds as possible.
Minutes 5-10: repeat 12 air squats and 8 V-ups for as many rounds as possible.
Minutes 10-15: repeat 6 burpees and 8 V-ups for as many rounds as possible.
Start a fresh score at each block and move directly to its first exercise when the clock changes. Record three separate rounds-plus-repetitions scores. Pace the full 15 minutes; step into and out of burpees or bend the knees during V-ups when needed.

For Time (15-minute cap, descending-repetition chipper)
- 60 Forward line hops: https://youtu.be/bL8mlpkp9Qo
- 45 Air squats: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rMvwVtlqjTE
- 30 Crunch: https://youtu.be/wY1zLTSm4VA
- 15 Burpees: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TU8QYVW0gDU
Complete each movement once in the listed order. Each landing counts as one hop. Record completion time or completed repetitions at the cap. Keep the full 15-minute segment available for completion, recovery and score recording.

Ladder (15-minute cap, ascending 3-6-9-12-15)
- Jump rope (single unders): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCuXYrTOMxI
- Push-ups: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_l3ySVKYVJ8
- Crunch: https://youtu.be/wY1zLTSm4VA
At each rung, perform 10 times the rung number of single unders, then the rung number of push-ups and crunches. Rope sets are 30, 60, 90, 120 and 150; push-up and crunch sets are 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15. Finish the ladder once and record time or completed repetitions at the cap. Use knee push-ups when needed.
